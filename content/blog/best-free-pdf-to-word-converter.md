## How to convert a PDF to Word for free

1. **Open [PDF to Word](/en/tools/pdf-to-docx/)** and add your PDF.
2. **Start the conversion.** The first time, your browser downloads the conversion engine, so it takes a little longer. Later conversions are quicker.

### Not sure if your PDF is a scan?

Open it and try to select a single word. If you can highlight it, the PDF has real text and will convert well. If the whole page highlights as one block, or nothing highlights, it's a scan. Run it through [OCR PDF](/en/tools/ocr-pdf/) first.

## Do you even need Word?

Sometimes converting is the long way round. If you only need to fix a typo, change a date or swap a name, you can do it directly in the PDF with [Edit PDF](/en/tools/edit-pdf/). Click into a paragraph and type, and the text re-wraps automatically. That keeps the original design exactly as it was, which conversion can't always guarantee.

A simple rule:

- **Small changes** to an otherwise finished document: edit the PDF directly.
- **Big rewrites**, reusing content, or working in tracked changes with others: convert to Word.

And when you're done editing in Word, [Word to PDF](/en/tools/word-to-pdf/) turns it back into a PDF, again without uploading it.

## Tips for the cleanest conversion

- **Use the original PDF, not a printed-and-scanned copy.** A PDF exported from a word processor converts far more accurately than a scan.
- **Remove the password first.** If the PDF is protected, unlock it with [Decrypt PDF](/en/tools/decrypt-pdf/) (you'll need the password).
- **Check tables and columns.** Complex multi-column layouts and tables come through, but spacing sometimes needs a quick tidy in Word.
- **Fonts may be substituted.** If the PDF uses a font you don't have installed, Word shows a similar one. The text is still fully editable.

## The honest trade-offs

- **The first conversion is slower** because the engine loads into your browser.
- **Heavily designed documents**, such as brochures with overlapping text boxes, convert with the content intact but may need layout adjustments. That's true of every converter.
- **Very long documents** take longer on older phones, since the work happens on your device.
