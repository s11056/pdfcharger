Printing a PDF should be easy, but scanned books with huge margins, documents mixing A4 and Letter pages, and slides that print tiny all get in the way. Here's how to fix the usual print problems for free.

## Crop away white margins

Scans and exported documents often have wide empty borders. Cropping them makes the text larger when printed and on screen, especially on tablets and e-readers.

1. Open [Crop PDF](/en/tools/crop-pdf/) on [PDFCharger](/en/).
2. Drop in your PDF and drag the crop box to the area you want to keep.
3. Tick **Apply to all pages** for a uniform crop, or adjust pages individually if they differ (for example left and right pages of a scanned book).
4. Download the cropped PDF.

**Tip:** leave a small margin around the text. Most printers can't print right to the edge of the paper, so a crop that's too tight may cut off letters.

## Make every page the same size

Merged documents often mix sizes: a Letter-size form, an A4 report, a landscape table. Printers then scale each page differently, or ask for different paper.

[Fix Page Size](/en/tools/fix-page-size/) converts every page to one size, such as **A4 or Letter**, or a custom size, and scales or positions the content to fit. Check the result with [Page Dimensions](/en/tools/page-dimensions/), which lists each page's size.

## Print several pages per sheet

Lecture slides, handouts and proofs often don't need a full page each. [N-Up PDF](/en/tools/n-up-pdf/) places **2, 4 or more pages on each sheet**, which saves paper and keeps related pages together.

## Other print fixes

- **Rotate sideways pages** with [Rotate PDF](/en/tools/rotate-pdf/) so landscape pages print the right way round.
- **Remove blank pages** left over from scanning with [Remove Blank Pages](/en/tools/remove-blank-pages/).
- **Save ink** by printing in greyscale with [PDF to Greyscale](/en/tools/pdf-to-greyscale/).
- **Print a large poster** across several sheets with [Posterize PDF](/en/tools/posterize-pdf/).
- **Lock form fields and comments** into the page with [Flatten PDF](/en/tools/flatten-pdf/), so they print exactly as they appear on screen.

## A good order for print preparation

1. Remove blank or unwanted pages.
2. Rotate any sideways pages.
3. Crop the margins.
4. Make all pages the same size.
5. Arrange several pages per sheet, if needed.
6. Print a single test page before printing the whole document.
