## What is a HEIC file?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the photo format iPhones and iPads have used by default for years. It keeps the same quality as a JPG at roughly half the file size, which is great for your storage, but support outside Apple devices is still patchy. Windows often needs an extra add-on, and many web browsers can't display HEIC at all.

## Tips for better document photos

- **Shoot straight on**, with the page flat and filling the frame.
- **Use daylight** and avoid your own shadow over the page.
- **Turn the flash off** for glossy paper, to avoid glare.
- **Prefer JPG in future?** On an iPhone, Settings > Camera > Formats > "Most Compatible" saves new photos as JPG instead of HEIC. Photos you've already taken stay HEIC.

## The honest trade-offs

- **HEIC decoding happens on your device**, so big batches are slower on older phones than on a laptop.
- **Live Photos and edits**: only the still image is converted, not the motion clip.
- **Converting doesn't sharpen a blurry photo.** Retake it if the text is hard to read.
