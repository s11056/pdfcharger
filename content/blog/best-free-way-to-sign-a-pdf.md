## Is an electronic signature legally valid?

In many countries, an electronic signature like this (an image of your signature placed on a document) is accepted for everyday agreements such as leases, offer letters and consent forms. Some documents, though, need a stronger certificate-based digital signature, or a handwritten one. The rules depend on your country and the type of document, so if it's important, check what the other party or your local regulations require.

If someone sends you a PDF with a certificate-based signature, you can check it with [Validate Signature](/en/tools/validate-signature/).

## Tips for a professional-looking signature

- **Draw slowly** on a trackpad or phone; fast strokes come out jagged.
- **Using paper?** Sign with a dark pen on plain white paper, in good light, and photograph it straight on.
- **Match the line.** Resize the signature so it sits on the signature line rather than floating above it.
- **Keep an unsigned copy** of the original, in case you need to sign again.
