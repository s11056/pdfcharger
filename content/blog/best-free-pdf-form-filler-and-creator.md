## Tips for forms people can actually fill in

- **Make text fields big enough** for real answers. Long addresses don't fit in a tiny box.
- **Use dropdowns for fixed answers** like country or department, so you get consistent replies.
- **Put the signature field last**, with a date field next to it.
- **Test your form** by filling it in yourself before sending it out.
- **Flatten the final copy** you keep on file, so the record can't be changed by accident.

## The honest trade-offs

- **Very complex dynamic forms**, such as some government forms built with older form technology, may not behave exactly as in the software they were designed for. Check the result before submitting it.
- **Form logic depends on the reader.** Desktop PDF readers generally support it; many mobile viewers don't.
- **Scanned forms have no fields.** Use the Form Creator to add them, or type on top with Edit PDF.
