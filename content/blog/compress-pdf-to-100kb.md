You've filled in an exam application, a job portal or a government form, and the upload box says: **"PDF only, maximum 100 KB."** Your scanned certificate is 2.4 MB. Sound familiar?

Here's how to get a PDF under an exact size limit, free, without handing your documents to a website.

## The quick way

1. Open the page for the size you need: [50 KB](/en/compress-pdf-to/50kb/), [100 KB](/en/compress-pdf-to/100kb/), [200 KB](/en/compress-pdf-to/200kb/), [300 KB](/en/compress-pdf-to/300kb/), [500 KB](/en/compress-pdf-to/500kb/) or [1 MB](/en/compress-pdf-to/1mb/).
2. Drop in your PDF. The target size is already selected.
3. Click **Compress PDF**, check the new size, and download.

Need a different limit? Open [Compress PDF](/en/tools/compress-pdf/) and type any size in KB into the **Target file size** box.

## How it reaches the target

The tool doesn't just apply one setting and hope. It works up a ladder:

1. **Gentle first.** It cleans up the file's structure and recompresses images at high quality. For many documents this alone is enough, and the text stays sharp and selectable.
2. **Stronger next.** It lowers image quality step by step.
3. **Last resort.** If the file still doesn't fit, it turns each page into an image and lowers the resolution until it does.

It stops at the first setting that fits, so you get the best quality that meets the limit.

## Why your file doesn't leave your device

Many of the documents people shrink for forms are sensitive: ID cards, mark sheets, caste or income certificates, bank statements. Most compression websites upload them to a server. [PDFCharger](/en/) compresses them **inside your browser**, so the file never leaves your phone or computer.

## Tips for very small limits (50–100 KB)

- **One page per file.** Many portals want each document separately. If your scan has extra pages, remove them with [Delete Pages](/en/tools/delete-pages/) or pull out the page you need with [Split PDF](/en/tools/split-pdf/) before compressing.
- **Scan in black and white or greyscale** if the portal allows it. Colour scans are much larger.
- **Scan at 150–200 DPI**, not 600. You won't see the difference on screen, but the file is far smaller.
- **Crop the edges.** Large white borders still cost bytes. [Crop PDF](/en/tools/crop-pdf/) trims them.
- **Check it's readable** before you submit. Zoom in on names, numbers and stamps.

## What if it can't reach the size?

A long document can't always fit into 50 KB and stay readable. If that happens, the tool tells you the smallest size it reached. Your options:

- remove pages the form doesn't need;
- split the PDF and upload the parts separately, if the portal allows several files;
- choose a slightly bigger target if the portal accepts it.

## A note on text

At the strongest setting, pages become images. They look the same and print fine, but you can't select or search the text any more. For form uploads that's rarely a problem; just keep your original file too.
