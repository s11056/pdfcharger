A small business handles more PDFs than most people realise: quotes, invoices, contracts, supplier agreements, payslips, client forms, brochures and reports. The usual answer is a PDF software subscription per person, which adds up fast for a team of three or ten.

In 2027 most of that work can be done for free. Here are the PDF jobs small businesses deal with most, and the free [PDFCharger](/en/) tools that handle each one.

## The privacy angle: why it matters more for businesses

When you upload a document to a typical online PDF site, you're handing your clients' names, addresses, bank details and contracts to a third party. In many places that comes with data protection obligations you'd rather not take on for the sake of merging two files.

Every PDFCharger tool runs **inside the browser**, so client documents never leave your computer. There's no account holding your files, nothing to breach on our side, and no third party to list in your records for routine document jobs.
