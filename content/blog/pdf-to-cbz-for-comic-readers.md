PDF is fine for reading comics on a laptop. But dedicated comic reader apps and home comic servers work best with **CBZ**: they show proper covers, sort issues into series, remember where you stopped, and handle right-to-left manga properly.

Here's how to convert PDF comics to CBZ, and back again, for free.

## What is a CBZ file?

A CBZ ("comic book zip") is simply a ZIP archive of page images, one image per page, in reading order. Because it's just images, comic readers open it instantly and page through it smoothly, even on older tablets and e-readers.

A good CBZ also contains a small **ComicInfo.xml** file with details such as the series, issue number, writer and publisher. Comic library apps read that file to organise your collection automatically, so you don't have to tag each issue by hand.

## Convert PDF to CBZ

1. Open [PDF to CBZ](/en/tools/pdf-to-cbz/) and drop in your PDF.
2. Fill in the details you want: **title, series, issue number, volume, writer, publisher and genre**.
3. For manga, set **right-to-left** reading so readers page in the right direction.
4. Choose the image format (**JPG, PNG or WebP**), the resolution and the quality.
5. Convert and download your `.cbz` file.

The tool renders every page as an image and writes the series details into the archive (as ComicInfo.xml and a metadata.opf file), so library apps can pick them up.

### Which settings to choose

- **JPG** at 85% quality is the best all-round choice: small files that look sharp.
- **PNG** keeps line art perfectly crisp but makes much bigger files.
- **WebP** is smaller than JPG at the same quality, but check that your reader supports it.
- **Greyscale** shrinks black-and-white manga noticeably, and suits e-ink readers.
- A higher **resolution** looks better on large tablets; a lower one saves space on phones.

## Convert CBZ to PDF

Going the other way is useful when a device or app only opens PDFs, or when you want to print a comic.

1. Open [CBZ to PDF](/en/tools/cbz-to-pdf/) and drop in your `.cbz` file.
2. Choose a page size: keep the original image size, or use a standard comic size.
3. Convert and download the PDF.

Pages stay in the correct reading order.

## Why convert in your browser?

Comic collections are big, and uploading hundreds of megabytes to a conversion website is slow. [PDFCharger](/en/) does the conversion **on your own device**, so there's no upload, no queue and no file size limit beyond what your computer can handle.

## Tips

- **Convert one volume at a time**, so each CBZ maps to one issue or volume in your library.
- **Name files consistently**, for example `Series Name 001.cbz`, so they sort in order.
- **Only convert comics you own.** These tools are for managing your own collection.

## The short version

To read PDF comics in a proper comic reader, convert them to CBZ with series details built in. To read a CBZ on a device that only takes PDF, convert it back. Both are free and run in your browser.
