Most students end up juggling a handful of half-free websites, each with its own sign-up, daily limit or watermark.

## Why students should care where their files go

Your coursework, ID, transcripts and financial aid documents are personal. Most online PDF sites upload them to a server to process them. Every PDFCharger tool runs **inside your browser**, so your files stay on your laptop or phone. It also means the tools work the same whether you're on a university computer, your own laptop or your phone.

## Submitting assignments

**Combine everything into one file.** Portals often accept a single upload. [Merge PDF](/en/tools/merge-pdf/) joins PDFs, Word documents, slides, spreadsheets and photos of handwritten work into one PDF, in the order you choose.

**Get under the size limit.** Upload portals and email often cap files at 10 or 20 MB. [Compress PDF](/en/tools/compress-pdf/) shrinks the file, and our guide to [compressing a PDF without uploading it](/en/blog/compress-pdf-without-uploading/) walks you through it.

**Add page numbers.** Many marking guidelines ask for them. [Page Numbers](/en/tools/page-numbers/) adds them in the position and format you need, and [Header & Footer](/en/tools/header-footer/) adds your name or student number to every page.

**Turn your Word essay into a PDF** with [Word to PDF](/en/tools/word-to-pdf/), so the formatting looks the same on your lecturer's screen as on yours.

## Studying and research

**Make scanned readings searchable.** Library scans and photocopied chapters are just pictures of text. [OCR PDF](/en/tools/ocr-pdf/) adds a searchable text layer, so you can press Ctrl+F to find a term, or copy a quote accurately instead of retyping it.

**Turn your highlights into notes.** If you annotate PDFs while reading, [Export Annotations](/en/tools/annotation-exporter/) pulls your highlights, comments and notes out into a Markdown or JSON file, organised by chapter. That's a ready-made revision document.

**Make references clickable.** [Citation Linker](/en/tools/citation-linker/) finds citation markers in a paper and turns them into clickable links or jumps to the right page.

**Pull out tables for your own analysis.** [Extract Tables](/en/tools/extract-tables/) exports tables from a paper as CSV, Markdown or JSON, and [PDF to Excel](/en/tools/pdf-to-excel/) gives you a spreadsheet.

**Read comfortably on your phone.** Papers designed for A4 are painful on a small screen. [PDF Layout Reflower](/en/tools/ai-pdf-reflower/) re-flows the text into a mobile-friendly layout, with Markdown and EPUB export.

## Saving paper and money

**Print several slides per page.** [N-Up PDF](/en/tools/n-up-pdf/) puts 2, 4 or more pages on each sheet, which is ideal for printing lecture slides as handouts.

**Print only what you need.** [Extract Pages](/en/tools/extract-pages/) or [Split PDF](/en/tools/split-pdf/) pulls out just the chapter you're reading.

**Got an e-book?** [EPUB to PDF](/en/tools/epub-to-pdf/) converts it so you can annotate it like any other reading.

## Editing and admin

**Fix a mistake without the original file.** [Edit PDF](/en/tools/edit-pdf/) lets you click into the text and change it directly.

**Need the content in Word?** [PDF to Word](/en/tools/pdf-to-docx/) turns a PDF back into an editable document.

**Fill and sign forms** for accommodation, internships and scholarships with [Form Filler](/en/tools/form-filler/) and [Sign PDF](/en/tools/sign-pdf/), with no printing or scanning.

**Sending a copy of your ID?** The [ID Composer](/en/tools/passport-id-composer/) puts the front and back of your card on one page and lets you watermark it with who it's for. Read [is it safe to upload your ID to a PDF website?](/en/blog/is-it-safe-to-upload-your-id-to-a-pdf-website/) first.

## Write in Markdown?

If you take notes or write reports in Markdown, [Markdown to PDF](/en/tools/markdown-to-pdf/) turns them into clean, formatted PDFs, including code blocks with syntax highlighting.

## A student-friendly checklist before you submit

1. Converted to PDF so the formatting is fixed.
2. Page numbers and your name or student number on every page.
3. Everything merged into one file in the right order.
4. Under the portal's size limit.
5. Opened once more to check nothing went missing.
