## Tips for a clean merged PDF

- **Name files before you add them**, such as "01 Application form" and "02 Passport". It makes ordering easy and gives you better bookmark names.
- **Photograph documents flat and in good light.** Merging doesn't improve a blurry photo.
- **Unlock protected PDFs first.** A password-protected PDF can't be merged until you remove the password with [Decrypt PDF](/en/tools/decrypt-pdf/).
- **Check orientation.** Rotate sideways pages in the [PDF Multi Tool](/en/tools/pdf-multi-tool/) after merging.

- **Complex spreadsheets** are converted as they would print, so wide sheets may be scaled down to fit the page.
